   `attest-desk` seals chain head + counts + canonical digests; the public bake

   lock note); the tailnet keeps every detail live. A member holding the detail can
   recompute the digests and catch a lying cloud. `skillsync-attest-publish` is the

   deeper fix when clicking away proved jarring: **the sky keeps you** — a star

8. **The herald.** `skillsync-herald` drafted in the drafts lane, approved,
   promoted, published (catalog 194), reflected into the corpus wiki, the master's

## What the day proved

- **The publication boundary holds everything**: leak scan, PII scrub, curation,
  proof-vs-detail — one boundary, many filters, sources never touched.
- **Walk evidence composes**: mouse-vault pathways, skill runtimes, garden
  registrations and counsel all seal into one chain a browser can verify.
- **The system consumes itself**: the kit ships in the site, the orientation
  serves from every door, the release rite is a packet, the desk carries its own
  guide. Nothing about the network is only in someone's head — or only on one
  machine.
