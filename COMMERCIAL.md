# Commercial licences

The agentprivacy skills corpus in this repository is offered to the public under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0
(see [LICENSE.md](./LICENSE.md)). Those terms exclude commercial use. This page says what a
commercial licence covers and how to obtain one.

## What counts as commercial use

Using a skill, persona, pattern, ceremony or deck from the corpus:

- inside a product or service that is sold, subscribed to, or used to generate revenue;
- inside a business, by employees or by agents acting for the business, including internal tooling;
- on behalf of a customer or client, or as part of paid consulting;
- in a fork or derivative set that is distributed commercially.

Running an agent with a skill loaded on a company's behalf is commercial use of that skill.

## What stays free

- reading, evaluating and trying the corpus before deciding;
- personal use, research, teaching and non-profit use, under the public terms;
- the Skill Sync tooling and every other piece of code in the repository, which is Apache-2.0 and
  may be run commercially without any further licence — the tooling is open; the corpus is what is
  licensed.

## What a commercial licence covers

A commercial licence is a written agreement with Mitchell Travers / 0xagentprivacy that grants, for a named licensee:

- use of the corpus, or a named subset of it, in products, services and internal operations;
- the right to adapt it for the licensee's own use without the share-alike obligation;
- redistribution to the licensee's customers as part of the licensed product or service;
- optional relief from the attribution requirement in the licensee's user-facing surfaces;
- a defined term, scope and fee, and support as agreed.

It does not transfer ownership, does not grant trademark rights (see [TRADEMARKS.md](./TRADEMARKS.md)),
and does not cover material the corpus quotes from third parties, which keeps its own terms.

## How to obtain one

Open an issue on this repository titled **"commercial licence"** describing the licensee, the intended
use and the packets or decks involved, or make contact through <https://agentprivacy.org>. Terms are
agreed in writing before use begins.
