Contributions are welcome — new skills, personas, patterns and decks, corrections, tooling.

  licences, so that the corpus can keep being offered to commercial users under one agreement; you

- you have the right to make that grant (it is your own work, or you hold permission for it), and you
  will name any third-party material you include so it can carry its own terms.

## Skills harvested from other gardens

Skill Sync also lists packets published by other members on their own farms. Those stay their
authors' and carry whatever terms the author declared in the packet's `license` field; a packet
with no declared terms is all rights reserved by its author and is listed, not copied onward.
